FREQUENT QUESTIONS

The essential details before you start.

How much does a business website cost?+

Starter projects start at $990, Business at $1,990 and Custom at $3,490 USD. Final price and taxes depend on approved scope and the applicable invoice.

Can you redesign my existing website?+

Yes. We review your current site, content and integrations before defining what can be reused and what needs replacement.

Can you help with domain and hosting setup?+

We can assist with deployment. Domain, hosting accounts and ongoing third-party fees are not automatically included.

Will I own my website?+

Ownership of original project deliverables and usage rights will be stated in the written agreement. Third-party software, fonts, libraries and services retain their respective licences.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance?+

Maintenance is optional and must be agreed separately; it is not an automatically included subscription.
