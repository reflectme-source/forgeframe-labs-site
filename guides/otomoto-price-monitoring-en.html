What events can you receive?

  • PRICE_CHANGE — observed asking-price change
  • NEW_LISTING — offer enters tracked results
  • REMOVED_LISTING — offer leaves tracked results
  • BASELINE_CREATED — first baseline saved

A listing disappearing does not prove a vehicle sale. Monitoring is periodic, not real-time.

04 / FAQ

Before your first scan.

Is this an official OTOMOTO API?

No. This is an independent Apify Actor that observes public search results. Check source terms and data rights before commercial use.

Are notifications real-time?

No. Events reflect differences between successive successful scans according to your schedule.

Does a removed listing mean a sale?

No. REMOVED_LISTING means only that the offer is absent from the monitored, bounded search result set.

Can I connect the data to a CRM?

Apify provides an API and datasets. A paid CRM integration requires an agreed receiver, testing and verification of data rights.

Need these events in your own system?

Describe the number of searches, scan frequency and target API. A custom CRM integration requires separate scoping.
