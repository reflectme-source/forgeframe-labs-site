# ForgeFrame Visual Critic

## Trigger
Use after art direction and after implementation; review independently of the implementation narrative.

## Review evidence
Inspect actual screenshots/rendered pages at 360, 768 and 1440px when a browser is available; review keyboard, pointer, touch, hover, focus and reduced-motion states. If browser unavailable, do not assert visual verification.

## Score out of 100
- Distinctiveness and art direction: 20
- Typography and layout rhythm: 20
- Product truth and comprehensibility: 15
- Microinteractions and motion discipline: 15
- Responsive and accessibility: 15
- Journey / conversion / trust: 10
- Performance and engineering polish: 5

Require >= 90/100 for design approval **and** no critical accessibility, security, dead-CTA, false-claim or missing-flow defects. A numeric score is a reviewer heuristic, not objective certification.

## Iteration
For each below-standard area give: screenshot/selector/viewport, precise issue, business impact, priority, and implementable change. Re-render and repeat up to 3 major iterations. Do not label a prototype or a mock screenshot "production tested".

Do not incentivize noise: fewer carefully chosen effects can rate better than impressive but distracting animation.
