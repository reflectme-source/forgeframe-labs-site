FAQ

Does it resend or modify invoices?

No. It analyzes supplied records without touching the ERP database or resubmitting documents.

Do I need to share KSeF credentials?

Not for an initial inquiry. Never send tokens, passwords or invoice contents to the contact email.

Does it support every ERP system?

No universal compatibility claim. Each source export and ERP version must be assessed.

How do we get started?
