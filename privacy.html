02. Data we process

Hosting may process IP addresses and technical logs. The contact form collects reply email, product, inquiry type and message, plus optional name, company and website. Orders may include billing details, order references and payment status. Do not submit passwords, tokens or unnecessary customer data.

03. Purposes and legal bases

We handle inquiries and pre-contract steps under GDPR Article 6(1)(b) where applicable, or legitimate interests in business correspondence (Article 6(1)(f)). We perform contracts under Article 6(1)(b), meet accounting and tax obligations under Article 6(1)(c), and protect systems and legal claims under Article 6(1)(f). Features requiring consent will request it separately.

04. Providers and international transfers

The website uses GitHub Pages. Contact form submissions go through FormSubmit to forgeframe.lab@gmail.com; direct email is an alternative. Payments may use Stripe. Individual products may use independent hosting and marketplaces such as Apify and other marketplaces, under their own policies. Some providers may process data outside the EEA; appropriate lawful transfer mechanisms, including adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses, are used where required.

05. Retention

Correspondence is kept as needed to respond and handle possible claims. Order and accounting records are kept for statutory periods. Technical logs follow reasonable security needs and hosting-provider rules. Data is deleted or anonymized when its lawful purpose ends.

06. Local processing and cookies

Localization QA Inspector processes CSV files locally in the browser and does not upload their contents to ForgeFrame Labs servers. The informational website currently uses no custom advertising pixels or marketing analytics. Infrastructure providers may use technical mechanisms necessary for service and security. Any new consent-requiring technology will be introduced with appropriate notice and choice.

07. Your rights

Subject to GDPR conditions, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, object to relevant processing or withdraw consent where consent applies. You may complain to the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO). Contact forgeframe.lab@gmail.com to exercise your rights.

08. Voluntary information and automated decisions

Providing form information is voluntary, but a reply address and inquiry details are necessary to respond. Some details are required for an order. The informational website does not make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects within GDPR Article 22.
