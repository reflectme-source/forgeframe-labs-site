The service

Atlassian offers free Connect-to-Forge tooling. We focus on the application-specific work those tools cannot complete for you: preserved behavior, permission boundaries and existing customer data.

Questions

Designed for private, business-critical extensions.

Does this replace Atlassian's free migration CLI?

No. We use the official tools where applicable and provide architectural analysis, risk ownership and client-specific acceptance criteria.

Does the assessment migrate our application?

No. Any build, staged installation or production cutover is contracted separately, after verifying technical feasibility and access.

Will our application data leave Atlassian?

No data needs to be sent for the first enquiry. Later assessment materials are transferred only through an agreed secure channel, with explicit confidentiality and retention terms.

What's the Connect deadline?

Atlassian has stated that Connect end of support begins on 31 January 2027. Private apps may face compatibility and security risks afterward; this is not a prediction of an immediate outage.
