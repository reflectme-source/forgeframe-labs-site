FAQ

Common questions.

Does it approve or reject Google products?

No. It produces a reviewed work queue; Google controls approval and enforcement.

Do you need my Merchant Center login?

No. Only authorized exports, transmitted via an agreed secure channel.

Is this just a free CSV matcher?

No. The service adds scoped review, exceptions and a human-readable handoff.

What is included in the report?

A scoped HTML summary, issue-to-SKU CSV and JSON output, plus documented exceptions.
