Before you request a quote

What this service does not claim.

Does it approve rejected Google products?

No. It organizes issues from supplied exports for human remediation. It is not a Google approval service, account appeal, live landing-page audit or guarantee of renewed ad delivery.

Do you need my Merchant Center login?

No. We start with CSV headers and a scope inquiry. Actual catalog data is handled only after authorization and an agreed secure exchange method.

Is this the same as a free CSV matching tool?

No unique CSV-join technology is claimed. The offered value is a human-reviewed, merchant-specific remediation handoff; if you already have that workflow covered internally, the service may not be useful.
