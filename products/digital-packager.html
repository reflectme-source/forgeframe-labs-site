FAQ

Before you download

Will Digital Packager upload my files?

No. Packaging is designed to run locally in your browser; we don't collect or store the files in your product folder.

Can it fit a 300 MB folder into one listing?

No. The published listing cap is five files of up to 20 MB each, and our tool doesn't degrade file contents or bypass those constraints.

Does the buyer need special software?

No proprietary joining tool is required. Each output ZIP is independent, and the buyer can extract each archive with common ZIP software.

Is this an Etsy product?

No. Etsy is a trademark of Etsy, Inc. This independently developed tool is neither affiliated with nor endorsed by Etsy.
