05 / FAQ

Questions about your monitoring.

Does this calculate my duty?

No. It identifies candidate legal documents and codes. Actual liabilities depend on the product, producer, additional codes, import date and applicable measures.

Does zero matches mean zero risk?

No. The feed is non-exhaustive and may be delayed or unavailable. Zero results cannot certify compliance.

Do I need to connect my ERP?

No. The public explorer doesn't require company-system access. Customer watchlists are planned, not available.

How do we activate monitoring?

Submit your tariff codes and countries of origin. Before taking payment, we confirm product scope, the activation date, responsibilities and subscription terms.
