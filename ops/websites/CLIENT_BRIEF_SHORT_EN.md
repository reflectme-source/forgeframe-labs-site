# ForgeFrame Labs — Website Project Brief (client-facing, EN)
Instructions: Please answer what you can. If something is unknown, write “not sure”. We will clarify only what matters for a quote.

1. **Business name and current website (if any)** — where is your company based, and what services/products do you offer?
2. **Main goal** — what should a visitor do: call, send an enquiry, book a consultation, visit your location, or request a quote?
3. **Pages and languages** — which pages do you need (Home, Services, About, Gallery, Contact, etc.) and in which language(s)?
4. **Features** — contact form, photo gallery, CMS-editable offers/menu, blog, bookings, translations or integrations? Please mention must-haves.
5. **Design direction** — brand colors, logo and 2–3 websites whose look you like; what do you want to avoid?
6. **Materials** — do you have approved copy, logos, photos and business details you have rights to use? Would you need us to prepare draft copy for approval?
7. **Domain and current email** — do you already own a domain or have an existing website? We do not need your passwords. Is business email hosted at that domain?
8. **Timing and budget** — desired launch period, approximate budget and your main point of contact.

We will respond with a fixed-scope quotation, project price, expected timing, revision policy and separate estimated ongoing domain/Webflow hosting costs, paid directly by you to the providers. Our standard service is one-time website creation, not a required maintenance subscription.

**Please don't email passwords, card details or sensitive personal data.**
