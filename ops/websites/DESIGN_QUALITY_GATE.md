# FORGEFRAME WEBSITES — DESIGN QUALITY GATE V2
Date: 2026-10-14
Owner feedback: FORMA Studio currently feels generic and recognizably AI-generated. The current demo is **rejected as commercial design quality**, regardless of whether the page is technically published. It is a technical delivery/CMS prototype only.

## Binding product decision
Do NOT market or sell the existing FORMA demo as proof of premium creative capability.
A working Webflow page is not sufficient to declare a customer website production-ready.
Site design/build methodology must be redesigned and validated before using it for paying clients.

## Design quality program (separate workstream)
- Collect client facts, genuine brand cues, conversion goals, audience and differentiators before design.
- Conduct sector-specific reference research; avoid automatically generated generalized layouts and inflated / invented marketing claims.
- Establish art direction, design concept, distinctive typography, imagery rights and design system before website implementation.
- Evaluate stronger coding/design skills, packages, vetted UI libraries and existing professional tooling; only adopt tools after benchmarking license, security, costs and maintainability.
- Create intentional bespoke information architecture, hierarchy, mobile design, motion restraint and interaction details.
- Independent design QA using screenshots for desktop/tablet/mobile; reject visible AI-template aesthetics, repetition and sloppy proportions.
- Formal editorial and technical QA: content provenance, accessibility, performance, SEO, responsiveness, production forms, broken links, licensed assets, no fake testimonials or fictitious company credentials.
- Gate: human sign-off on visual quality before any commercial publication or prospect sharing. Failed design returns to creative direction, not polish patches.

## Work split
Stream A: high-end design and construction — OPEN, must be re-engineered.
Stream B: post-build delivery (payments, domain, hosting, content-editor access, launch, handover) — can be operationally piloted assuming Stream A is accepted, but does not change demo's rejected design status.
