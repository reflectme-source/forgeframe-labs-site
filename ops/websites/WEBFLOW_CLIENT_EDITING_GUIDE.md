# Webflow — Client content editing guide (pilot)

This guide applies to the FORMA Studio example and may need adapting to each customer's plan and roles.

## Test the editable CMS
1. Sign into https://webflow.com/dashboard using an account permitted to access the site.
2. Open **ForgeFrame Demo 01**.
3. In CMS, open **Concept Projects**.
4. Open **Still House**, **Atelier Residence**, or **Quiet Geometry**.
5. Edit a safe field: **Project Overview**, **Name**, **Discipline**, or **Project Image**.
6. Save and publish the item using the Webflow publishing controls.
7. Open https://forgeframe-demo-01.webflow.io/ and check the **The Living Archive / CMS** section.
8. Undo an undesired test modification, then publish again.
9. New CMS items should appear through the live Collection List (dynamic query; max 3 records displayed at once).

## Real customer delivery
- Prefer client-owned billing and domain; do not register customer domains to an agency-only account.
- Confirm Webflow plan needed for CMS/Content editor and publishing rights; do not assume free access covers a customer launch.
- Grant access through Webflow invitations using least-privilege role; do not share passwords.
- Provide one short video and ask customer to make a safe sample edit while supervised.
- Record hosting payer, domain registrar / renewal owner, project ownership, the scope of warranty, content rights, and exit/handover procedure.
- Do NOT edit Stripe Commerce Core, ForgeFrame's existing paid products, or DNS until explicitly scoped and authorized.

## Client-ready service principle
Client can change approved text, photos, services, and portfolio items without asking the agency for each update. Structural redesign or complex integrations require a change order.

## Note on the demo
FORMA is a fictional concept and is not a real interior architecture firm. The site does not accept customer enquiries into a dedicated fictional mailbox.
