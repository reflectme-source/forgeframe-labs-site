## What the customer can manage
- Log in at https://webflow.com/dashboard using own account.
- Open the transferred site, enter Webflow content editing interface.
- Edit approved page text, hyperlinks and images.
- For service menus, case studies or blog entries: CMS panel > collection > item > edit fields > save/publish.
- Confirm changes on live domain in a private/incognito browser.
- Webflow's content editing cannot redesign layouts or install new features. Those are new paid development orders.
- Keep own registrar and billing information current. Webflow sends provider and plan notifications directly.
