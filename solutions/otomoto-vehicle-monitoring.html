Designed for a specific job

Market observations, not another full listing scraper.

Otomoto Change Intelligence records a bounded snapshot of a public search result set and compares it with the previous successful snapshot. It supports targeted monitoring rather than collecting every field on every vehicle advertisement.

  • PRICE_CHANGE: observed asking-price or currency change
  • NEW_LISTING: offer entered your tracked search set
  • REMOVED_LISTING: offer disappeared from that result set
Control usage

Know what each scan can cost.

The Actor uses Apify pay-per-event pricing. The public listing currently displays pricing from US$10 per 1,000 source scans, with additional event charges possible. Actual charges depend on scanned pages and emitted events.

Review Apify's current pricing and your maximum run-charge limit before starting. The first run creates a baseline and may not contain historical price-change events.

The Actor monitors public OTOMOTO result pages. It does not guarantee complete marketplace coverage, vehicle availability or instant notifications. Alert delivery requires your configured schedule and integration.
Common questions

What to expect from the first run.

Why are there no price changes yet?

Your first successful scan establishes the baseline. A later successful run with the same search URL and snapshot key is needed to compare changes. Enable current-snapshot output if you want to inspect existing offers on the first run.

Does “removed” mean a car was sold?

No. It means the offer was no longer present in the monitored public search result window. It may have moved outside your selected filters, moved past the page limit or otherwise disappeared from those results.

Can I monitor a different region or model?

Yes. Choose your filters on the public OTOMOTO search website and use its resulting URL in the Actor. Use a stable snapshot key for recurring comparisons.

Will the Actor send email alerts automatically?

It produces structured dataset events. Use Apify Schedules and your own webhook, workflow platform or notification tool if you want delivered alerts. No standalone email subscription is included.

Is this an official OTOMOTO service?

No. ForgeFrame Labs operates an independent public-search monitoring tool distributed on Apify. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or an official API of OTOMOTO.
