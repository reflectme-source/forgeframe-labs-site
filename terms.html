Professional setup — service terms

These terms describe ForgeFrame Labs' optional, manually delivered setup service for ForgeFrame Contract Guard. The underlying GitHub Action is free to self-host and does not require a purchase.

$149 USD one-time / one eligible GitHub repository. Before payment, we verify technical scope, agree on delivery and identify the legal seller in writing. There is no automatic subscription or hosted SaaS access.

Seller and contact

ForgeFrame Labs is the public brand for independent software services operated from Poland. The responsible legal seller, applicable billing information and agreed service details are disclosed in the individual written scope confirmation before payment. Request these details at forgeframe.lab@gmail.com. Do not pay until the seller and scope are clear.

Included in the fixed-scope setup

  • One GitHub repository with a valid existing OpenAPI 3.x specification in YAML or JSON.
  • A pull-request GitHub Actions compatibility gate, using an audited immutable Action reference.
  • Configuration of the baseline and a documented policy for explicit, time-limited exceptions.
  • A demonstration of an additive change passing and a breaking change being blocked.
  • Transfer of JSON/Markdown release evidence and concise operating instructions.

Excluded: building an API from scratch, modifying live server behavior, migration of multiple repositories, cloud hosting, ongoing monitoring, and certification of regulatory or runtime compatibility. Additional work requires a separate written quote.

Eligibility, ordering and delivery

Contact us before paying. We check specification format, repository permissions, CI runner compatibility, and any security constraints. We confirm a realistic delivery date and agreed scope by email. Payment is then taken through a Stripe-hosted page in USD. Bank exchange rates may vary. Work begins only after confirmed payment and approved least-privilege access. If we cannot accept a proposed repository or schedule, do not pay.

Do not email access tokens, passwords, secrets, confidential source files or full private API specifications. Any repository access must be granted through the platform's ordinary permission controls and may be revoked on completion.

Cancellation, refunds and statutory rights

If ForgeFrame Labs cannot deliver the agreed service, contact forgeframe.lab@gmail.com so the payment can be reviewed and refunded to the original payment method when appropriate. Requests received before work starts will normally receive a full refund. Once work begins, the outcome depends on the agreed scope, work completed and mandatory applicable law.

The setup offer is designed primarily for business customers. Any mandatory consumer protections — including applicable withdrawal rights — remain unaffected. We do not assume a customer has waived rights merely by paying. Where the law requires express consent to start services during a withdrawal period, that consent must be obtained separately.

Limitations and support

Contract Guard analyzes changes recorded in OpenAPI specifications. It does not test actual runtime compatibility, detect all undocumented behavior, or guarantee that downstream customers will not experience problems. We provide the agreed configuration and handover; a request for correction can be sent to forgeframe.lab@gmail.com.

Payments and privacy

Stripe processes card and payment-method details on its own hosted checkout. We receive only the transaction, customer and billing information necessary for fulfillment, customer support and legally required recordkeeping. See the ForgeFrame Labs privacy notice and Stripe privacy statement.
